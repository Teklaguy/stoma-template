Stoma Template

Measure a stoma from a photo and print a true-size cutting template.

Take a photo with a bank card beside the stoma

  • Lay a clean bank card or driving licence flat on the skin next to the stoma, not touching it. Wipe it or put it in a clear bag first.
  • Hold the phone straight above the skin, parallel to it, close enough to fill the frame. Good light, no flash glare.
  • Keep the whole card and the whole stoma in view.

If “Take photo” does nothing on your phone, tap “Choose from library” and pick “Take Photo” from the menu that appears.

About this tool

Trial version for evaluation (version 1.0, October 2026). It is not a medical device and has not been clinically validated. Do not rely on it alone to size an appliance: check against the manufacturer's sizing guide and use clinical judgement.

How it works. You photograph the stoma with a bank card (85.6 x 53.98 mm) lying flat beside it. The card's four corners give the scale and correct for perspective. You trace the stoma outline, and the app prints a true-size template as a PDF.

Accuracy. It depends on a flat card, a sharp photo and the phone held parallel to the skin. In tests on simulated photos the card corners were found to within about 1 pixel, which is a small fraction of a millimetre at normal distances. It has not been compared with clinical measurements on real patients.

Privacy. The photo is processed inside this page and is never uploaded. A patient name, saved measurements and suppliers are stored only in this browser on this device. Nurses trying it out should leave the patient name blank.

A guide only, not a medical device. Your photo is processed inside this page and is never uploaded. Check the fit with your stoma care nurse and your product's sizing guide before cutting.

Saved measurements

    Saved in this browser only: the outline in millimetres and the name you entered, never the photo. Clearing site data removes them.
